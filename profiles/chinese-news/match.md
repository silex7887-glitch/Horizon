# Chinese news profile

Use this profile for reporting from Chinese-language newsrooms, including news about China, Hong Kong, Taiwan, and technology or public affairs written for a Chinese-reading audience.

Typical items include politics and policy in the Chinese-speaking world, cross-strait or regional developments, and substantive Chinese reporting on science, companies, or society.

Do not use this profile for English-language world or conflict reporting; that belongs to world affairs. Do not use it when the primary subject is markets, inflation, interest rates, earnings, or company finance; that belongs to financial news. A Chinese-language business story stays here when the newsroom and audience are Chinese. An English market story stays in financial news even if it is about a Chinese company.
