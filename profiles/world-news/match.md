# World affairs profile

Use this profile when an item's primary value is politics, diplomacy, armed conflict, security, or the humanitarian and institutional consequences of those events.

Typical items include elections and transfers of power, wars, ceasefires, sanctions, territorial disputes, major treaties, refugee or civilian-protection crises, and diplomatic realignments.

Do not use this profile for product releases, research, or engineering developments; those belong to technology news. Do not use it when the primary subject is markets, inflation, interest rates, earnings, or company finance; those belong to financial news. A conflict that also moves markets stays here. A central-bank or budget decision stays in financial news even if politicians comment on it.
